# Acme Corporation — Strategic Portfolio & Technology Architecture (2025)

## Executive Leadership & Governance
Acme Corporation is led by Chief Executive Officer Marcus Vance, who was appointed in January 2023. Marcus Vance previously served as Senior Vice President of Engineering at NeuralSphere. Under his leadership, Acme Corporation expanded its AI infrastructure division, culminating in the strategic acquisition of CyberCore Technologies in September 2024.

Following the acquisition, Dr. Elena Rostova was named Lead Research Scientist at Acme Corporation. Dr. Elena Rostova directs the Quantum AI Lab located in Zurich, Switzerland, reporting directly to Chief Technology Officer Sarah Chen.

## Technology Ecosystem & Intellectual Property
CyberCore Technologies developed the HyperVector neural indexing engine, which enables billion-scale vector similarity search. At Acme Corporation, the HyperVector engine was integrated into the enterprise flagship platform known as ApexCore.

Dr. Elena Rostova and Senior Principal Engineer David Kim co-authored the patented Dynamic Graph Embedding Algorithm, which connects ApexCore to distributed knowledge graph backends. Acme Corporation partners with CloudScale Global for international datacenter hosting and compliance certification across the European Union.
